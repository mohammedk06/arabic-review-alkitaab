2 · Pronouns — the master table
Subject pronouns + the one suffix set that does double duty as object of a verb and possessive on a noun — the single highest-leverage table in the course.

💡 THE ONE DISTINCTION
On a noun the “I” suffix is ـي (my book = كتابي). On a verb/preposition it's ـني (helps me = يساعدني). Same idea, one extra ن on the verb.
3 · The noun phrase
3a–3b · Definiteness & Idafa (L5, L3)
Definiteness: Indefinite = tanwīn (كتابٌ / كتابًا / كتابٍ). Definite = الـ prefix (الكتاب); sun letters assimilate (الشمس → ash-shams). A noun is already definite (no الـ) if it has a possessive suffix (كتابي) or is the second term of an idafa.
Idafa الإضافة: Noun1 (no الـ, no tanwīn) + Noun2 (definite) = “N1 of N2.” “The family's house” = بيتُ العائلة — only the last noun takes الـ. Adjectives describing an idafa go after the whole chain. “Prime minister” = رئيس الوزراء (head of the ministers).
3c–3d · Plurals & the dual (L3, L6)
TypeRuleExample
Sound masculineـون / ـينمُدرِّس → مُدرِّسون
Sound feminineة → ـاتمُدرِّسة → مُدرِّسات
Broken (most common)pattern change — memorize per wordكتاب → كُتُب ; دولة → دُوَل
Dual المثنى: exactly two → suffix ـان (subject) / ـين (object/after prep): كتابان / كتابين ; طالبتان.
3e–3g · Nisba, demonstratives & agreement (L2, L5)
Nisba: add ـيّ / ـيّة → “relating to”: دَولة → دُوَليّ (international); مصر → مصريّ.
Demonstratives: هذا this (m) · هذه this (f) · ذلك that (m) · تلك that (f). With a definite noun: “this book” = هذا الكتاب.
Agreement: adjectives follow the noun and match in gender, number, definiteness, case. Non-human plurals take a feminine singular adjective (كتب كثيرة, not كثيرين).
4 · Sentence types
Nominal الجملة الاسمية (L4)
mubtada' + khabar, no verb “to be”
الطقسُ حارٌّ
“The weather [is] hot.”
Verbal الجملة الفعلية (L4)
Verb + subject (verb often first, stays singular)
يدرسُ الطلابُ
“The students study.”
Fronted predicate الخبر المقدّم (L7)
khabar before mubtada' — “there is / I have”
عندي أخٌ
“I have a brother.”
Descriptive clause جملة الصفة (L11)
Defines an indefinite noun — no الذي/التي
تعرّفنا على امرأة تعمل في قسم اللغات
“…a woman who works in the languages dept.”
📏 THE DEFINITE / INDEFINITE SPLIT
Definite noun → link with الذي / التي (spoken اللي). Indefinite noun → link with nothing = جملة الصفة. Either way the clause needs a عائد (linking pronoun) pointing back at the noun — hidden inside the verb if the noun is the subject (امرأة تعمل), written out if it's an object (مباراة استمتعت بها · سوق ذهبت إليه).
Reading clue: اسم نكرة + verb = almost always a جملة صفة.

6 · Root & patternالجذر والوزن ⚙️
The engine of Arabic. Every word = a root (usually 3 consonants carrying core meaning) woven into a pattern (وزن).
How to find the root: (1) strip prefixes/suffixes and الـ; (2) drop long vowels (esp. alif, but not hamza) and pattern letters س، ت، م، ن; (3) what's left (usually 3 consonants) is the root.
دُخول د-خ-ل
مشغول ش-غ-ل
وزارة و-ز-ر
أكبر ك-ب-ر
مستقبل ق-ب-ل
الاقتصاد ق-ص-د
💡 TWO GOTCHAS
• Shadda = a doubled root letter: عمّ → ع-م-م ; صفّ → ص-ف-ف.
• Weak letters show up in the plural or the مضارع: خال → أخوال (reveals و) ; عاش → يعيش (reveals ي).
المصدر (verbal noun): the “-ing / to-do” noun from a verb — دَخَلَ → الدُخول. Used after أراد: أريد الدخولَ = أريد أنْ أدخلَ.
The verb patterns — أوزان الفعل (L11 makes this a system)
Awzaan aren't a list to memorize — they're a network. Three principles: (1) each wazn's meaning relates to other awzaan, not to an abstract reference point; (2) the longer the wazn, the more abstract the meaning (shadda = extension/intensification); (3) each wazn pairs with one or two others in form and meaning — II & V share a shadda, III & VI share an alif.
Waznماضي · مضارع · مصدرSense / marker
Iفَعَلَ ، يَفعَل ، الفَعْلbase meaning
IIفَعَّلَ ، يُفَعِّل ، التَفعيلshadda · causative of I
IIIفاعَلَ ، يُفاعِل ، المُفاعَلةalif · doing it to someone
IVأفعَلَ ، يُفعِل ، الإفعالinitial hamza
Vتَفَعَّلَ ، يَتَفَعَّل ، التَفَعُّلتـ + shadda · reflexive of II
VI ★تَفاعَلَ ، يَتَفاعَل ، التَفاعُلتـ + alif · reciprocal — the "each other" wazn
VII ★اِنفَعَلَ ، يَنفَعِل ، الانفِعالنْ ("seve-n") · passive of I; rare
VIIIاِفتَعَلَ ، يَفتَعِل ، الافتِعالinfixed تـ
Xاستَفعَلَ ، يَستَفعِل ، الاستِفعالاستـ · most abstract
The four pairings to own (L11):
• I → II = causative: عَرَفَ (know) → عَرَّفَ بـ (introduce)
• II → V = transitive → reflexive: عَرَّفَ → تَعَرَّفَ على (get acquainted) · ذَكَّرَ بـ → تَذَكَّرَ (recall)
• I → VII = passive: قَطَعَ (cut) → اِنقَطَعَ (be cut off)
• III → VI = reciprocal: تَبادَلَ (exchange with each other)
⚠ V vs. VI is the live confusion: both start with تـ and are all-fatḥa. V has a shadda (تَجَمَّعَ، تَعَرَّفَ); VI has an alif (تَبادَلَ). L11's vocabulary list contains both.
Bonus: II and V can be built from a noun — سوق → سَوَّقَ (to market) / تَسَوَّقَ (to go shopping).
7 · Numbers, comparison, conditionals
Numbers 11–100 (L5): the notorious reverse-agreement zone. 11–99 the counted noun is singular accusative (عشرون طالبًا). Re-drill separately.
Ordinals (L9): الأول، الثاني، الثالث…. “at 6:30” = في السادسة والنصف.
Comparative أفعل (L10): أكبر، أصغر — invariable, followed by مِن.
Superlative أفعل (L7): same form + idafa or الـ: أكبر مدينة / الأكبر.
Conditionals (L10): إذا = real/likely (“if/when”); لو = hypothetical/counterfactual.
