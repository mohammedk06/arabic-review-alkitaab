A note on the L1–L7 verbs' past forms

Al-Kitaab presents L1–L7 verbs in the present tense (past-tense grammar isn't taught until Lesson 8; L9–L10 give the full ماضي/مضارع/مصدر trio directly). Where the book showed present only, the الماضي column gives the standard dictionary/citation forms — not invented, just supplied for consistency. Many مصدر forms are book-confirmed (العَمَل L2; السَفَر L4; الحُصول, الجَري, السِباحة, الرَقص, التَصوير L6; and the L9–L10 trios verbatim).

🧭 The Verb Forms (الأوزان) — rules & patterns

Note

Pattern columns use ف-ع-ل as the model root: ف = R1, ع = R2, ل = R3. "Mine" = verbs from the table above.

FormالماضيالمضارعالمصدرWhat it doesMine
Iفَعَلَ / فَعِلَيَفعُل · يَفعِل · يَفعَلunpredictable — memorize itThe bare root meaning, no extra layerدَرَسَ، كَتَبَ، فَهِمَ
IIفَعَّلَيُفَعِّلتَفعيلCausative or intensive of I — "make/cause someone to do X", or do X repeatedlyدَرَّسَ (to teach = make study)، دَخَّنَ
IIIفاعَلَيُفاعِلمُفاعَلة (also فِعال)Doing X to / with someone else — directed at a partnerسافَرَ، قابَلَ، ساعَدَ
IVأَفعَلَيُفعِلإفعالCausative like II, but often making something be in a stateأصبَحَ، أرادَ، أيقَظَ
VتَفَعَّلَيَتَفَعَّلتَفَعُّلReflexive of II — the action turned back on the doerتَكَلَّمَ، تَخَرَّجَ، تَذَكَّرَ
VIتَفاعَلَيَتَفاعَلتَفاعُلReciprocal of III — two or more parties doing it to each otherتَبادَلَ
VIIاِنفَعَلَيَنفَعِلاِنفِعالPassive / intransitive of I — it happens to the subject, no agent namedاِنقَطَعَ
VIIIاِفتَعَلَيَفتَعِلاِفتِعالReflexive of I, often doing X for one's own benefitاستَمَعَ، التَحَقَ
IXاِفعَلَّيَفعَلّاِفعِلالColors and physical defects only (اِحمَرَّ = to turn red)none yet
XاِستَفعَلَيَستَفعِلاِستِفعالSeeking or considering X — "ask for / find something to be X"استَطاعَ، استَمتَعَ

Recognition shortcuts:

  • Present prefix vowel: Forms II, III, IV take يُـ (ḍamma). Everything else takes يَـ (fatḥa). This alone splits the field in half.
  • تَـ at the front of the past → V or VI. Doubled ع = V, long ا = VI.
  • ا at the front of the past (اِنـ / اِفـ / اِستـ) → VII, VIII, or X — these all drop the alif in the present.
  • المصدر is only unpredictable in Form I. Forms II–X follow the patterns above every time, so you only have to memorize مصدر for Form I verbs.
  • Hollow roots (middle و/ي: قالَ، كانَ، نامَ، عاشَ) still count as Form I — the weak letter hides the pattern, it doesn't change it.
