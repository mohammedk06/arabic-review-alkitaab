جدّتي توقظني في السادسة والنصف
My Grandmother Wakes Me at Six-Thirty
◆ WHAT THIS LESSON LOCKS IN
The indicative present (المضارع المرفوع) — the formal, “main-verb” mood that is the فصحى counterpart of the spoken بـ prefix — and the ordinal numbers (الأعداد الترتيبية) used to tell time (في السادسة والنصف = “at six-thirty”). Theme: the daily routine.

💡 ROOT PAYOFF
The meals line up by root and daypart: الفُطور (breakfast, ف-ط-ر — same root as فَطَرَ “break the fast”) · الغَداء (lunch) · العَشاء (dinner). And ع-و-د keeps giving: عادَ (return), العَودة, عادةً (usually), مُعيد (TA, L6) — all “coming back / repetition.”
📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. بعض (“some of”) heads an iḍāfa with a plural noun/pronoun: بعض الناس ينامون بعد الظهر ; بعض زملائي.
2. نَزَلَ / يَنزِل means both “go out (of the house)” and “stay (in a hotel)” — the preposition disambiguates: النُزول من البيت (leave the house) vs. النُزول في فندق (stay at a hotel).
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a · The indicative — المضارع المرفوع
When a مضارع verb is the main verb of its clause, formal Arabic puts it in the مرفوع (indicative) mood — the فصحى equivalent of the spoken بـ prefix (بيدرُس → يَدرُسُ). Two visible markers:
Personsمرفوع markerExample
أنا / أنتَ / هو / هي / نحنfinal ‑ُ (ḍamma)أفعَلُ · يفعَلُ · نفعَلُ
أنتِ / أنتم / همthe ن endings ‑ينَ / ‑ونَتفعَلينَ · تفعَلونَ · يفعَلونَ
You mainly hear the ‑ُ in very formal speech (news broadcasts). The other moods — منصوب (L10) and مجزوم (L13) — strip or change these endings.
2b · Ordinal numbers — الأعداد الترتيبية
Ordinals mark order/place in a sequence. Pattern = فاعِل (from the cardinal's root), except “first”:

2c · Telling time — كم الساعة؟
Formal Arabic tells time with the feminine ordinal (الساعة is feminine) — exception: 1:00 uses الواحدة. Fractions: والنصف (½), والربع (¼), والثلث (⅓), إلا ربع (¼ to).

The lesson title — في السادسة والنصف (“at six-thirty”) — is exactly this pattern in action.
3 · Culture & readings
Story: خالد's daily routine — his grandmother wakes him at 6:30, structuring the whole day's vocabulary (meals, prayer times, going out, coming back).

Culture — morning greetings: صباح الخير / صباح النور / صباح الفل — the ritualized reply chain.

Reading — من برامج التليفزيون: a TV schedule (times, program names) — perfect for ordinal-number + time practice.
4 · Common pitfalls
1Dropping the ن on أنتِ/أنتم/هم in the indicative — main-verb تفعَلينَ / يفعَلونَ keep the ن (that's what مرفوع means).
2Using a cardinal for the hour in formal Arabic — it's الساعة الثانية (ordinal), not الساعة اثنان.
3Forgetting 1:00 is the exception — الساعة الواحدة, not الأولى.
4Mismatching gender on ordinals — with الساعة (f) use the feminine ordinal (السادسة).
5Following بعض with a singular — بعض takes a plural noun in an iḍāfa (بعض الناس).
