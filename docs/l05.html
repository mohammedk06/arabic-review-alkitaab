الطقس حار جداً في الصيف
The Weather is Very Hot in the Summer
◆ WHAT THIS LESSON LOCKS IN
Definiteness in phrases vs. sentences (المعرفة والنكرة) — the الـ that separates “the big city” from “the city is big” — plus the demonstratives هذا/هذه, the numbers 11–100 with their singular-noun rule, and how tanwīn al-fatḥa (‑اً) turns a noun into an adverb. Theme: weather and seasons.

💡 ROOT PAYOFF
ح-ر-ر links حارّ (hot), الحَرارة (heat), دَرَجة الحَرارة (temperature) — and even حُرّ (free) sits on the same root. The season/adjective pairs are a mini-system: الصَيف→صَيفيّ، الشِتاء→شِتَويّ (nisbas from L1 doing quiet work).
📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. أحسَن + an indefinite noun = “the best ___”. Behaves like a noun here — no الـ, no ة: أحسَن جامعة (the best university), أحسَن فَصل (the best season).
2. بالنِسبة لـ = “as far as ___ is concerned” (opinion). Attach the pronoun suffix: بالنِسبة لي / لكَ / لها.
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a · Definite vs. indefinite — المعرفة والنكرة
A noun-adjective phrase and a verbless sentence look almost identical — the only difference is whether the adjective carries الـ:
Structureالـ on adj?Example
indefinite phrasenoneمدينة كبيرة (a big city)
definite phraseon bothالمدينة الكبيرة (the big city)
sentencenoun onlyالمدينة كبيرة (the city is big)
Everything in one phrase must match in definiteness. A verbless (nominal) sentence = definite مبتدأ + indefinite خبر.
2b · Demonstratives — هذا / هذه
Same word, two constructions — the difference is again الـ:
ConstructionExampleMeaning
+ indefinite nounهذه مدينة كبيرةthis is a big city
+ definite noun (الـ)هذه المدينة الكبيرةthis big city
هذا = this (m) · هذه = this (f).

⚠ THE COUNTED-NOUN RULE
Unlike 3–10 (plural noun), 11–99 take a SINGULAR noun — in formal Arabic accusative with tanwīn (‑اً), like after كَم؟: عندي عشرون كِتاباً (20 books) ; خمسة عشر مُوَظَّفاً (15 employees). Exactly 100 also takes a singular noun, but in the genitive (مئةُ كتابٍ). The classic trip-wire — re-drill separately.
2d · Adverbs from tanwīn al-fatḥa — تنوين الفتح
Add the ending ‑اً to a noun/adjective to make an adverb (describes a verb). Most spell it with a final alif:
جِدّاً very
كثيراً a lot
قليلاً a little
أحياناً sometimes
دائماً always
جَيّداً well
Reading tip: a non-proper-noun ending in a bare alif is very often an adverb — read it with the ‑اً ending. A few adverbs end in ة instead (which never takes an alif).
3 · Culture & readings
Story: the family discusses the weather across seasons — the title الطقس حار جداً في الصيف is the anchor sentence.

Culture: small-talk norms around weather; offering/declining (لا.. ولا حاجة).

Reading — نيويورك: a descriptive text about a city (weather + place vocab). Song: فيروز، «حبيتك بالصيف» — seasons in a cultural register.
4 · Common pitfalls
1Confusing “the big city” with “the city is big” — المدينة الكبيرة (الـ on both) vs. المدينة كبيرة (الـ on noun only).
2Forgetting الـ after هذا for “this X” — هذه المدينة (this city), not هذه مدينة (= “this is a city”).
3Pluralizing the noun after 11–99 — it must be singular (عشرون كتاباً, not عشرون كتب).
4Skipping the ‑اً on adverbs — it's جِدّاً / كثيراً, and that ending is what makes it adverbial.
5Putting الـ or ة on أحسَن — أحسَن جامعة, not الأحسن جامعة.

warm-upQ3. Turn قَليل into an adverb.
Reveal answer
قَليلاً — add tanwīn al-fatḥa (‑اً).
adverb ‑اً (قَليلاً)
applyQ4. Give both: “the hot weather” (phrase) and “the weather is hot” (sentence).
Reveal answer
الجَوُّ الحارُّ (phrase) / الجَوُّ حارٌّ (sentence) — الـ on the adjective = phrase; noun only = sentence.
phrase vs sentence (الـ on adjective)
applyQ5. Translate both: “This is a new house.” and “this house” (m).
Reveal answer
هذا بيتٌ جديدٌ (this is a new house) / هذا البيتُ (this house) — no الـ = “this is a ___”; add الـ = “this ___”.
"this is a ___" vs "this ___" (الـ)
applyQ6. Which is the correct full sentence for “The house is new”: البيتُ جديدٌ or البيتُ جديداً? Why?
Reveal answer
البيتُ جديدٌ — the خبر (predicate) is nominative ‑ٌ. The ‑اً ending is only for adverbs (جِدّاً) and counted nouns, never a predicate.
predicate case ‑ٌ not ‑اً
combineQ7. Translate: “This is a big city, but the weather is very hot in summer.”
Reveal answer
هذه مدينةٌ كبيرةٌ، ولكنَّ الطَقسَ حارٌّ جِدّاً في الصَيف.
multi-clause: demonstrative + predicate + جِدّاً
combineQ8. Translate: “My friend (f) is Egyptian, and she sometimes feels lonely.”
Reveal answer
صَديقتي مِصريّةٌ، وتَشعُرُ بالوَحدةِ أحياناً. — مِصريّة agrees f; صَديقتي is definite (possessed), so it needs no هذه.
gender agreement + possessed noun
combineQ9. Translate: “In winter, the temperature is twenty degrees.”
Reveal answer
في الشِتاء دَرَجةُ الحَرارةِ عِشرون دَرَجةً. — 11–100 take a singular counted noun in the accusative ‑اً (here ‑اً is correct — contrast with Q6).
number 11–100 + singular ‑اً
combineQ10. Fix every error (intended meaning: “This is a big city”): هذه المدينة كبيرةً.
Reveal answer
هذه مدينةٌ كبيرةٌ. — (1) drop الـ: with المدينة it means “this city” and the sentence breaks; (2) كبيرةٌ is nominative (predicate), not كبيرةً.
fix errors: definiteness + predicate case
