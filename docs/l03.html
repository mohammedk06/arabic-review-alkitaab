LESSON 3 Al-Kitaab Part One (3rd ed.)  ·  pp. 39–62  ·  ↩ General Review
عائلة والدي كبيرة
My Father's Family is Big
◆ WHAT THIS LESSON LOCKS IN
The iḍāfa (الإضافة) — Arabic's noun-stacking way to show possession — plus the possessive-suffix set (ـي، ـكَ، ـهُ…), the feminine plural (ـات), and a new broken-plural pattern (فُعَل). Together these are the machinery of the whole noun phrase; the title itself, عائلة والدي, is an iḍāfa.

💡 ROOT PAYOFF
ع-ل-م is a goldmine: عِلم (science), عُلوم (sciences), عالِم (scholar), مُعَلِّم (teacher), مَعلومات (information), عَلامة (mark/sign). Watch how علم + a second noun builds whole disciplines: عِلم الإنسان, عِلم النَفْس, العُلوم السياسية — that's iḍāfa doing the work (§2d).
📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. أولاد / وَلَد covers children of either gender (وَلَد · ج. أولاد = boy/child · children, mixed).
2. كَم is always followed by a singular noun. Formal puts it in the accusative with tanwīn (‑اً); spoken drops it: كم طالِباً في الصَفّ؟ (formal) / كام طالِب في الفَصل؟ (spoken).
3. Family terms come in مذكّر / مؤنّث pairs — learn each pair together: عَمّ / عَمّة (paternal uncle/aunt), خال / خالة (maternal), جَدّ / جَدّة (grandfather/grandmother).
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a–2b · Feminine plural (ـات) & the فُعَل pattern
Nouns/adjectives for groups of human females are regular: singular ة → plural ـات.

⚠ THE “WOMAN” EXCEPTIONS
Memorize: امرأة → نِساء ; مَرّة → نِسوان ; سِتّ → سِتّات.
New broken pattern — وزن فُعَل: صورة → صُوَر ; دَولة → دُوَل ; أُمّة → أُمَم.
2c · Nonhuman plural agreement — feminine singular
The rule that surprises everyone: a plural of nonhuman things takes a feminine singular adjective.
PhraseLiterallyNote
سَيّارات يابانِيّة“Japanese cars”يابانية = fem. singular
لُغات أوروبِيّة“European languages”fem. singular adj.
جامِعات كبيرة“big universities”fem. singular adj.
2d · Iḍāfa — الإضافة (the construct chain)
Two (or more) nouns placed together to show possession / belonging — English “N1 of N2”:
جامعة نيويورك
New York University
مَكتَب القَبول
the admissions office
قِصّة المَرأة
the woman's story
شُغل البَيت
housework
RuleDetail
First noun: bareno الـ, no tanwīn — definite by definition
Last noun: carries definitenessonly the final noun takes الـ or a suffix
Adjectivesdescribe the whole chain, go after it
2e · Possessive suffixes — ضمائر الملكية
Possession on a single noun = attach a suffix (really a one-noun iḍāfa). Shown on بَيت:

هو / هيـهُ / ـهابَيتُهُ / بَيتُها
نحن / أنتم / همـنا / ـكُم / ـهُمبَيتُنا / بَيتُكُم / بَيتُهُم
⚠ ة → ت UNDER A SUFFIX
When a suffix attaches to a word ending in ة, the ة is written and pronounced as ت: جَدّة → جَدَّتي (my grandmother), عائلة → عائلَتي.
3 · Culture & readings
Story: خالد walks through his large extended family — the title عائلة والدي كبيرة sets up the whole vocabulary of relatives.

Reading — Arab universities: short profiles of جامعة بيروت العربية and جامعة حلب; practice reading institution names (many are iḍāfas).

Activity — the Prophet's family: a chart naming relationships within عائلة النبي محمد (fathers, sons, wives, grandsons الحسن والحسين) — pure relationship-vocabulary drill.
4 · Common pitfalls
1Putting الـ on the first word of an iḍāfa — it must be bare (عائلة والدي, never العائلة والدي).
2Giving nonhuman plurals a plural adjective — سَيّارات يابانِيّة (fem. sing.), not يابانيّات.
3Forgetting ة → ت under a suffix — it's جَدَّتي, not جَدّةي.
4Trying to derive broken plurals — صورة → صُوَر follows فُعَل, but you memorize which words take it.
5Following كَم with a plural — كَم takes a singular noun (كم طالِباً, not كم طُلّاب).
