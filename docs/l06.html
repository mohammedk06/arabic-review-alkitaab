💡 ROOT PAYOFF
ع-و-د (return/repeat) is why a TA is a مُعيد (“one who goes back over” material) — same root as عادَ (return), عادة (habit), أعادَ (to repeat). And the hobby words are almost all مَصادِر (verbal nouns): الجَري, السِباحة, الرَقص, التَصوير — exactly the grammar point in §2b.
📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. يَحصُل على has a narrow meaning: to obtain from an institution — a degree, a job, a scholarship. Not “get” in general.
2. تَخَرَّجَ is a past-tense verb (subject suffix ‑تُ: تَخَرَّجْتُ “I graduated”), even though full past grammar comes in Lesson 8.
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a · The dual — المثنى
For exactly two, Arabic has a dedicated dual suffix — not “2 + plural.” Two forms in فصحى (by role); one in most dialects. Root of “two” = ث-ن-ي (اثنان, المُثَنّى):
RoleSuffixExample
Subject (مرفوع)‑انبيتان ; سَنَتان
Object / after prep.‑ينبيتَين ; سَنَتَين
In فصحى the dual also shows on adjectives, verbs, pronouns (هذان البيتان صغيران ; هما أختان تدرسان). For now, recognize the ‑ان / ‑ين endings.
2b · The maṣdar — المصدر (verbal noun)
The مصدر is the abstract “to-do / -ing” noun of a verb — literally “the source” (مَصدَر · ج. مَصادِر). It has no tense, is almost always definite, and often heads an iḍāfa:
VerbمصدرUsed as
حَصَلَالحُصولأدرُس للحُصول على ماجستير
سافَرَالسَفَرأُحِبّ السَفَر
تَخَرَّجَالتَخَرُّجبعد التَخَرُّج
First look at verb patterns (الأوزان) — the مصدر is predictable from the Form:
FormوزنمصدرExample
IIفَعَّلَالتَفعيلدَرَّسَ → التَدريس
IIIفاعَلَالمُفاعَلةساعَدَ → المُساعَدة
Vتَفَعَّلَالتَفَعُّلتَخَرَّجَ → التَخَرُّج
⚠ مصدر ≠ ENGLISH “-ing” ALWAYS
“Running is a sport” (noun → مصدر ✅). “I am running” (ongoing → not a مصدر; that's the verb أجري). A مصدر is a noun — it can't take a subject.
2c · Giving a reason — لماذا؟ / لأنّ / لـِ / بِسَبَب
Your first complex (multi-clause) sentences. لماذا؟ is answered three ways, each with its own grammar:
ConnectorFollowed byMeaning
لأنّa full جملة اسميةbecause
لـِمصدر or مضارعin order to / for
بِسَبَبa noun (iḍāfa)because of
Attachment rules: a pronoun after لأنّ attaches to it (لأنّها مريضة ; also لأنّي / لأنّكَ / لأنّهُ…). And لـِ + الـ drops the alif of الـ (لـِ + القِراءة → للقِراءة).

4 · Common pitfalls
1Using “2 + plural” instead of the dual — it's بيتان / سَنَتَين, not اثنان بيوت.
2Treating the مصدر as an ongoing action — it's a noun; “I am running” needs the verb, not الجَري.
3Putting a verb right after لأنّ — it needs a جملة اسمية (noun first): لأنّها مريضة, not لأنّ تكون مريضة.
4Following بِسَبَب with a sentence — it takes a noun only (بسبب الازدحام); for a clause use لأنّ.
5Forgetting لـِ + الـ contraction — للحُصول / للدراسة, not لالحصول.
6Over-using يَحصُل على — it's for obtaining from an institution, not “get” in general.
