كيف أحفظ كل الأسماء؟!
How Do I Memorize All the Names?!
◆ WHAT THIS LESSON LOCKS IN
The present tense (المضارع) as a full conjugation system, the idea of verb patterns (الأوزان / Forms I–X), how to negate the present (لا), the object-pronoun suffixes (ضمائر النصب), and the formal names for the two sentence types — nominal (اسمية) vs. verbal (فعلية). The grammar-heaviest lesson of the batch.

💡 ROOT PAYOFF
أ-ك-ل (eating) and ق-ر-أ (reading/reciting) both keep a hamza as a real root letter — a consonant, not decoration. When أ-ك-ل conjugates in the “I” form, two hamzas collide and fuse into an alif madda: آكُل (“I eat”). And ط-ع-م ties مَطعَم (restaurant) to طَعام (food) and طَعِمَ (to taste).
📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. قَبلَ before a verb needs a linker. Before a noun it attaches directly (قبل السفر). Before a verb, spoken adds ما (قبل ما أنام); formal adds a linking word (fully in Lesson 8).
2. السَفَر is a noun (verbal noun), not an adjective. You can say “I love traveling,” but not “I am traveling” — a مصدر can't express an ongoing action. Use the verb أسافِر for that.
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a · The present tense — الفعل المضارع
المضارع covers present and habitual and ongoing (“I study / am studying / do study”). The subject rides on the verb via a prefix (± suffix); the separate pronoun is optional.
PronounPrefix/suffixيَدرُس
أنا / أنتَ / أنتِأ‑ / تـ‑ / تـ‑…ـينأدرُس / تَدرُس / تَدرُسين
هو / هييـ‑ / تـ‑يَدرُس / تَدرُس
نحن / أنتم / همنـ‑ / تـ‑…ـون / يـ‑…ـوننَدرُس / تَدرُسون / يَدرُسون
Main vs. dependent verbs: in spoken Arabic a بـ prefix marks a main verb (بيدرُس); a verb following another verb drops it (بدي أدرُس). Formal مضارع uses no بـ.
2b · Form I stem vowels — الوزن
Prefixes/suffixes are constant, but the stem vowel of a Form I verb varies and must be memorized per verb — three types:
يَفعُل
ḍamma stem
يَدرُس (studies)
يَفعَل
fatḥa stem
يَعمَل (works)
يَفعِل
kasra stem
يَعرِف (knows)
Form I is the only pattern with a variable stem vowel — Forms II–X are regular. See the full engine in General Review › Root & pattern.
2c–2d · Negating the present & object pronouns
Negation: put لا directly before the مضارع verb — لا نَدرُس الفرنسية ; لا يَعمَل في المكتب ; لا تَتَذَكَّر اسمي.
Object pronouns (ضمائر النصب): the third pronoun set — identical to the possessive suffixes except “me” (ـني on a verb vs. ـي on a noun):
ObjectSuffixExample
meـنييُدَرِّسني (teaches me)
him / herـهُ / ـهاأحفَظهُ / لا أتَذَكَّرها
us / you-pl / themـنا / ـكُم / ـهُميُساعِدنا / يُساعِدكُم / أعرِفهُم
2e · Sentence types — الاسمية والفعلية
Two ways to build the same idea; the difference is word order and analysis, not meaning:
TypePartsExample
Nominal الاسميةمُبتَدأ + خَبَرزوجتي تَستَمِع إلى الراديو
Verbal الفعليةفِعل + فاعِلتَستَمِع زوجتي إلى الراديو
In a nominal sentence the خَبَر tends to be indefinite (a clue for where the predicate starts), and مبتدأ/خبر agree in gender/number. الاسمية fronts a topic; الفعلية fronts the verb.
3 · Culture & readings
Story: مها, overwhelmed meeting Khalid's huge family, wonders how she'll memorize everyone — the hook for a lesson about names and daily routine.

Culture — الأكل العربي: vocabulary and norms around meals; food is a language of hospitality.

Reading — مطعم باب الحارة: a restaurant text — menu/food vocab in context. Dialogue: ordering and offering drinks (تشربوا حاجة الأول؟).
4 · Common pitfalls
1Guessing the Form I stem vowel — يَدرُس (ḍamma) vs. يَعمَل (fatḥa) vs. يَعرِف (kasra) must be memorized.
2Negating the present with ما — that's for the past; the present takes لا.
3Dropping the ن on أنتم / هم — تَدرُسون / يَدرُسون keep the ‑ون in the plain indicative.
4Using ـي for “me” on a verb — it's ـني (يُدَرِّسني), not ـي.
5Treating السَفَر like “-ing” as an action — it's a noun; you can't say “I am traveling” with it.
6Miscounting hamza — in أكَلَ and قَرَأَ the hamza is a root letter.
