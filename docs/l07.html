◆ WHAT THIS LESSON LOCKS IN
The superlative (أفعل التفضيل) — “the biggest / best X” — and the fronted predicate (الخبر المقدّم), i.e. how Arabic says “I have / there is,” including the crucial عند vs. لـ vs. فيه split. Plus a first, light look at the future (سـ). Register: loss and condolence.

📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. “Sad” splits by pattern. A person who feels sad = Form I state (حَزين / زَعلان); a thing that makes you sad = Form II/active (مُحزِن).
2. عامّ = the public sector, not “public school”: المكتبة العامّة (public library), العمل العامّ (public service), الإدارة العامّة (public administration).
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a · The superlative — أفعل التفضيل
You know two أفعَل words: أكبَر (from كبير) and أحسَن (from حسن). Pattern = أفعَل (two syllables, fatḥa vowels, initial alif). For a superlative, put أفعَل first, as head of an iḍāfa, followed by an indefinite noun:
أكبَر بلد في أوروبا
the biggest country in Europe
أحسَن جامعة
the best university
ألطَف رجل
the nicest man
📏 In one line: Superlative = أفعَل + indefinite noun (no الـ on the noun). The comparative use of the same word is a separate construction — see Lesson 10 §2b.
2b · Fronted predicate — الخبر المقدّم (“I have / there is”)
“I have” and “there is/are” don't use a verb — they front a predicate (a preposition phrase) before the subject. The word depends on the possessor:
PossessorUseExample
a humanعند / لـعندي أخ · لي خالة
place / institutionفي · فيه · فيهابيتي فيه أربع غرف
very abstractلـ (not عند)لهذه المدينة تاريخ طويل
لـ is more formal/abstract than عند. عند is human-only — a house or university “has” rooms/professors with في/فيه, never عند. This is a nominal sentence with the خبر moved up front.
2c · The future — المستقبل (light)
Attach سـ (or the word سوف) to a مضارع verb: سأذهب معك إلى المستشفى (I will go with you to the hospital) ; سوف نتخرّج هذه السنة (we will graduate this year).
3 · Culture & readings
Story: the family learns of a death (الله يرحمها) — the vocabulary and etiquette of loss and condolence.

Culture — condolence language: fixed pious phrases (رَحِمَها الله / الله يرحمها) that punctuate speech around death; there's a right thing to say.

Culture — الثانوية العامة: the high-stakes secondary-school exam system (ثانوي / إعدادي / ابتدائي tiers). Reading — النعي: obituary/death-notice prose (formal register).
4 · Common pitfalls
1Putting الـ on the noun after a superlative — أكبَر بلد, not أكبَر البلد (that changes the construction).
2Using عند for a place — a house “has” rooms with فيه (بيتي فيه…), never بيتي عنده.
3Confusing مُحزِن with حَزين — مُحزِن = causes sadness (news); حَزين = feels sad (person).
4Forgetting the سـ is attached — it's سأذهب (one word), not سـ أذهب.
5Reading عامّ as “public school” — it means the public sector; schools are a different word.
