📎 Notes on usage (from the lesson)
1. Words for “family” take singular agreement (like English “the family is”): أُسرَتي ساكِنة في بوسطن (my family lives in Boston); عائِلَتي ساكِنة في منطقة الجيزة.
2. Time expressions: في vs. بـ. Formal & Egyptian favor في; Levantine often uses بـ. Both fine: في النَهار (daytime), في اللَيل (night). Spoken attaches directly: بالنَهار / باللَيل.
2 · Grammarالقواعد
2a · Subject pronouns — الضمائر
Arabic has three pronoun sets (subject, object, possessive). This lesson activates the full subject set, including plurals:
أنا

أنتَ
you (m)
أنتِ
you (f)
هو

هي

نحن

أنتم

هم

Formal Arabic also has dual and feminine-plural pronouns (أنتما، هما، أنتنّ، هنّ) — most spoken dialects skip them; they come later.
2b · Plural verb conjugation — الفعل في الجمع
You knew the singular present (أدرُس، تدرُس، يدرُس). Here are the three common plurals on يَعمَل (“to work”):
PronounيَعمَلEnding
نحننَعمَلنـ‑
أنتمتَعمَلونتـ‑ + ‑ون
هميَعمَلونيـ‑ + ‑ون
أنتم mirrors أنتَ (both تـ‑), and هم mirrors هو (both يـ‑) — plus the plural ‑ون.
2c · Noun plurals — جمع الاسم
Arabic doesn't just add “s.” Store singular + plural together. Three routes:
TypeRuleExample
Sound masc. (humans only)‑ون / ‑ينمُتَرجِم → مُتَرجِمون
Sound fem. (L3)ة → ‑اتجامِعة → جامِعات
Broken (majority)pattern change — memorizeبَلَد → بِلاد ; مَركَز → مَراكِز
From L3 on, the vocab list gives every noun's plural — learn both forms as one unit.
3 · Culture & readings
Story: مها feels وحيدة — cut off from her extended family in Egypt while living in the US.

Culture — being alone: in Arab culture, spending long stretches by yourself (outside work/study) is generally seen as undesirable; frequent visits among neighbors, extended family, and friends mean one is rarely alone for long. That frame is what makes مها's loneliness the emotional hook.

Reading — العائلة العربية: a formal passage on how the rural extended-family structure grew out of land-based economic production and kin solidarity — your first taste of dense expository فصحى.
4 · Common pitfalls
1Pluralizing “family” — أُسرة / عائلة take singular agreement (أُسرَتي ساكِنة, not ساكِنات).
2Using ‑ون/‑ين for nonhumans — the sound masculine plural is humans only.
3Trying to predict broken plurals — بَلَد → بِلاد isn't derivable by rule; memorize with the singular.
4Mismatching plural verb endings — أنتم and هم both end in ‑ون (تَعمَلون / يَعمَلون); don't drop the ن.
5Over-thinking في vs. بـ for time — both acceptable; just don't mix registers.
